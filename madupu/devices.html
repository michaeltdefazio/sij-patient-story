Two devices from Omnia Medical

Omnia Medical makes the PsiF90 and the PsiFGuard. They are designed to work together in SI joint fusion done from the back. Here is each one in plain words. Dr. Madupu will talk with you about which approach fits your anatomy and your goals.

The implant kit

PsiF90implant kit

Purpose
A sterile, surgery-ready kit with the implant and the tools to place it.
Path
In from the back, directly into the SI joint.
Incision
About the size of a dime, as described by Omnia Medical.

The sound guide

PsiFGuarddrill

Purpose
A drill that gives sound feedback while your doctor places the first guide wire.
Sound
Different tissues make different tones, so your doctor can tell joint space from bone.
Status
FDA cleared, according to Omnia Medical and its maker, SpineGuard.

How the sound guide works

As the drill moves, PsiFGuard measures how well the tissue in front of it conducts electricity and turns that into a tone your doctor can hear. Your doctor also relies on touch and on what they see.

High tone
Soft tissue, such as blood or the joint space.
Low tone
Harder structures, such as the dense outer layer of bone.

What to keep in mind

  • This is information from the device maker. It is not a study of patients.
  • Omnia refers to a laboratory study done on cadavers. Results in real people can differ.
  • Many options exist for SI joint pain. The right choice depends on your anatomy and your doctor’s judgment.

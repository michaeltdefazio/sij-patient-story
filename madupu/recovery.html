Recovery after SI joint fusion

A general guide. Timing varies, and Dr. Madupu’s team gives you your own plan.

What many people can expect

This is a general timeline from Omnia Medical for SI joint fusion with the PsiF system. Every person heals at their own pace, so Dr. Madupu’s team will give you your own plan.

  1. Surgery day

    Going home

    Many people leave the surgery center or hospital the same day.

    Most can walk within hours. A cane, crutches or a walker can take stress off the joint while it fuses.

  2. The first week

    Taking it easy

    Keep the incisions dry and the dressing on until your first visit, at about 1 week. Take sponge baths and avoid driving long distances.

    At that visit, your doctor decides whether you can return to work with light activity.

  3. Weeks 2 to 4

    Getting back to your routine

    Many people return to work and light activity in about 2 to 3 weeks.

    Your second visit is at about 4 weeks. Until then, do not lift more than 10 pounds. Take pain medicine as prescribed. Ice can calm inflammation, and heat can ease muscle tension and spasms.

  4. Up to 12 weeks

    Back to full activity

    Avoid running and jumping for 12 weeks.

    Many people can return to full activity at about 12 weeks.

In shortMany people go home the same day and walk within hours. Light activity often returns in about 2 to 3 weeks, and full activity at about 12 weeks.

Call Dr. Madupu’s office if you notice:

  • A fever above 101°F
  • Bleeding from the incision
  • Signs of infection: leaking, swelling, redness or discoloration, a bad odor, or warmth at the incision
  • New pain, or pain that is getting worse

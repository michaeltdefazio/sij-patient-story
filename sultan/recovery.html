What many people can expect

This is a general timeline for ultra minimally invasive SI joint fusion at Wellspring Pain Solutions. Every person heals at their own pace, so Dr. Sultan’s team will give you your own plan.

  1. Surgery day

    Going home

    You go home the same day. Many people stop for lunch on the way home.

    Walking starts soon after surgery.

  2. The first days

    Taking it easy

    Pain is expected at first and usually eases within a week.

    Follow Dr. Sultan’s instructions for activity, and call the office with any questions.

  3. Weeks 2 to 4

    Getting back to your routine

    Most people recover in about 2 to 4 weeks.

    Dr. Sultan’s team tells you when you can return to work, driving and exercise. If physical therapy is prescribed, they tell you when to start.

  4. The following months

    Continued healing

    The joint keeps strengthening as it heals.

    Tell Dr. Sultan’s team if your pain returns or gets worse.

In shortYou go home the same day. Pain usually eases within a week, and most people recover in about 2 to 4 weeks.

Call Dr. Sultan’s office if you notice:

  • A fever above 101°F
  • Bleeding from the incision
  • Signs of infection: leaking, swelling, redness or discoloration, a bad odor, or warmth at the incision
  • New pain, or pain that is getting worse
